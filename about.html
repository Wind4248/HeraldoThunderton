About the Attorney

44 Years of Protecting Columbus Ohio Families

Lorelei M. Lanier has spent her entire legal career dedicated to one mission: helping Central Ohio families protect what matters most. Since earning her Juris Doctor from Capital University Law School in Columbus, she has built one of the region's most trusted boutique estate planning practices — right here in Upper Arlington on Riverside Drive.

What sets Lorelei apart is not just her depth of knowledge, but the way she practices law. In an era of large, impersonal firms, she has deliberately kept her practice small — because she believes every client deserves direct, personal attention from their attorney. When you hire Lorelei, you work with Lorelei.

A Practice Built on Personal Connection

Estate planning touches some of the most important moments in a family's life — preparing for the future, caring for aging parents, and navigating the loss of a loved one. Lorelei approaches every client relationship with warmth, patience, and a commitment to making sure you understand every step of the process.

Clients consistently describe her as easy to reach by phone, flexible with scheduling, and willing to go the extra mile — even meeting clients in their homes throughout Upper Arlington, Dublin, Bexley, New Albany, and the broader Columbus area when travel is difficult.

Deep Expertise in Ohio Estate Law

With four decades of practice in Ohio, Lorelei has an unmatched command of Ohio probate law, trust administration, elder law planning, and estate tax strategy. She has guided hundreds of Columbus-area families through:

  • ✓ Drafting legally airtight wills and last testaments under Ohio law
  • ✓ Creating revocable and irrevocable trusts that avoid probate and protect assets
  • ✓ Guiding executors through Franklin County Probate Court
  • ✓ Medicaid planning for seniors facing long-term care costs
  • ✓ Drafting durable powers of attorney and healthcare directives
  • ✓ Business succession planning for Columbus-area business owners

Recognition & Reputation
