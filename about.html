About the Attorney

44 Years of Protecting Columbus Ohio Families

Lorelei M. Lanier has spent her entire legal career dedicated to one mission: helping Central Ohio families protect what matters most. A graduate of The Ohio State University and Capital University Law School, she has built one of the region's most trusted boutique estate planning practices — right here in Upper Arlington on Riverside Drive.

What sets Lorelei apart is not just her depth of knowledge, but the way she practices law. In an era of large, impersonal firms, she has deliberately kept her practice small — because she believes every client deserves direct, personal attention from their attorney. When you hire Lorelei, you work with Lorelei.

A Practice Built on Personal Connection

Estate planning touches some of the most important moments in a family's life — preparing for the future, caring for aging parents, and navigating the loss of a loved one. Lorelei approaches every client relationship with warmth, patience, and a commitment to making sure you understand every step of the process.

Clients consistently describe her as easy to reach by phone, flexible with scheduling, and willing to go the extra mile — even meeting clients in their homes throughout Upper Arlington, Dublin, Bexley, New Albany, and the broader Columbus area when travel is difficult.

Deep Expertise in Ohio Estate Law

With four decades of practice in Ohio, Lorelei has an unmatched command of Ohio probate law, trust administration, elder law planning, and estate tax strategy. She has guided hundreds of Columbus-area families through:

  • ✓ Drafting Last Will and Testament under Ohio law
  • ✓ Creating revocable living trusts to avoid probate and protect vulnerable family members
  • ✓ Guiding executors through Franklin County and other Ohio county probate courts
  • ✓ Medicaid planning for seniors facing long-term care costs
  • ✓ Drafting durable powers of attorney and healthcare directives

Recognition & Reputation

Lorelei has earned a "Distinguished" peer review rating from Martindale-Hubbell — awarded to attorneys with high professional achievement and ethical standards as recognized by their peers. She has also received Martindale-Hubbell's Client Champion Award, reserved for attorneys whose clients affirm exceptional service through verified reviews.

On Google, Avvo, and legal directories, she holds a 4.8-star rating across 20+ client reviews, with clients consistently praising her expertise, accessibility, and genuine care.

Transparent, Flat-Fee Pricing

Lorelei believes legal services should be accessible and predictable. After your free 30-minute consultation — by phone or in person — she provides a firm, flat fee for preparation and execution of estate planning documents. No billable hours, no surprise invoices. You'll know exactly what your estate plan will cost before any work begins.
